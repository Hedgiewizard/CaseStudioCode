“The Best Chocolate Chip Cookie Recipe” Redesign

Designer: Grace Lewis

Problem Statement

How do we keep the website accessible while also keeping it monetizable?

Objectives & Goals

My Objectives

My objective is to improve the design and make it more user-friendly while also making the business happy and keeping it monetizable.

Audience Objectives

For this specific site, they want a site that will help teach them how to make chocolate chip cookies.

Business Objectives

Have a product that people use, and that makes the company money.

Do these objectives clash?

  1. The biggest clash I am seeing is the user’s desire for a less busy, more usable website and Joy Food Sunshine (JFS)’s objective of being able to place ads and make money.
  2. Another issue the people I talked to specifically had was that there was too much rambling before the website. One of the user testers I asked for help didn’t realize there was a walk-through of the recipe before the actual recipe because it just looked like a wall of text and pictures to them. On the topic of photos, the photos all take up almost the entirety of the screen and there are a lot of them making it difficult to just find the text of the recipe in the walk through. I think the placement of images needs to become more intentional.
  3. Another problem we had was that the organization of the content can feel a bit random and not justified at times. This made it harder to tell if you were reading information that you needed to finish the recipe, or it was just fun to know information.
  4. The website also feels very crowded. Although I think that might be in part be because of the problems listed above and that fixing them will make the site feel less crowded.

Business Challenges

  • This site is the creator’s (Joy Food Sunshine(Laura)) primary source of income. Their main challenges are how to make the site as monetizable as possible while also getting people use the site.

Product Users & Changes

Who is using this site?

Mom’s of younger kids, probably in their mid 20s – early 30s. They know how to cook and are just looking for a new recipe

User Needs

  • Better Usability

User Challenges

  • From talking to people, I learned that most people’s main problems were that cooking websites like this felt too crowded. It felt like the website had too much fluff and not enough actual content.
  • Another problem a lot of the people I talked to was that there were far too many ads for the site to be usable
  • I didn’t notice this at first because my ad blocker blocked most of them, and so I thought they were just videos that hadn’t fully loaded yet.
  • The content also felt very disorganized to most. It was hard to find the content they were there for. One of my users didn’t notice the site had a walkthrough outside of the recipe at the bottom. They just thought that was a part of the wall of “filler text,” as they put it

Features & Functionalities

My main goal with this redesign was to simplify the content Laura originally had(cut down on when she was saying things three or four times) and reorganize the content to make it easier to navigate.

Competitor Analysis

Since this is just a redesign, the “competitor” in question is just the original site.

Things that are liked

  • It is nice that she had some photos of things that people might not fully understand from just the words, such as what they should look like when coming out of the oven.
  • I liked that there was a way to jump to the recipe. I do wish the button were a bit more visible.

Things that are disliked

  • It feels like a lot of the site is a wall of text.
  • There were a decent number of pictures that felt more like filler than something that was needed.
  • The content felt unorganized.

Notable Changes

  • Instead of having all of the headers be the same size I changed the header sizes to be: Section headers (like the how to walkthrough, ingredients and substitutions, more recipes to try, and the FAQ), Step headers(like “steps for making the dough”, “how to portion and roll”, etc.), Sub-step headers (like how to freeze the dough and such).
  • Condensed information. Went through and tried to make it so any piece of information wasn't mentioned more than twice.
